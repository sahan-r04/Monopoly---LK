yetToMake.txt
==============
What still needs to be built so the whole MONOPOLY-LK simulation fully matches
the assignment brief. Organized by file. Updated after completing every gap
that was previously listed here: round-tick systems (depreciation, condition
decay, insurance countdown, recurring Luxury Tax, group cooldowns, damage
repair retries), railway/utility live rent, Income Tax, Rule-LK 35 effect
reversion for every economy-level system, the Economic Events deck (Rule-LK
18), Rule-LK 36's market display, and Anti-Speculation/Labour Strike
enforcement. The full project compiles and links with zero warnings
("gcc *.c -o monopoly -Wall -Wextra") and a full 500-round run completes
without crashing.

Status at a glance:
- types.h    - complete
- board.c    - complete (movement, rent-by-development-level, square reopening)
- finance.c  - complete (loans/insurance/rent/tax/net-worth/bankruptcy/all
               round-tick systems)

               reversion paths that are in scope - see below for the one

What's left is a small number of deliberately bounded, documented gaps -
each one is explained in assumptions.txt, not silently missing.


Known, documented gaps (not oversights - see assumptions.txt for the
reasoning behind each)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- No card, regulation, or market effect (National Event Cards, Regional
  Development Cards, Government Regulations, Market Boom/Decline, Economic
  Events) reverts its price/rent change once its roundsRemaining runs out -
  every one applies correctly when drawn, then stays in effect permanently
  for the rest of the simulation, the same simple way Inflation already
  works. This was a deliberate simplification (an exact reversal system was
  built and then removed) to keep the logic easy to explain and defend; see
  assumptions.txt #33 for the full reasoning.
- Business Interruption Insurance's "5 rounds of lost rental income"
  compensation (Section 1.2) isn't tracked - only the repair-cost portion of
  its payout is paid. Would need a per-square rounds-remaining counter and a
  round-tick handler, neither built (no assumptions.txt entry - this one is
  a straightforward remaining gap, not a judgment call).
- Rule-LK 32's Market Decline "-25% auction starting price" isn't wired into
  runAuction() (game.c) - the auction's opening bid is always 50% of
  currentValue (Rule-LK 19) regardless of whether the property's group is
  currently in a Market Decline. currentValue itself IS reduced by Market
  Decline already, so the auction price is indirectly lower, just not by
  the full stated -25% on top of that.
- The Anti-Speculation Act's 3-undeveloped-property cap is only enforced in
  decideToPurchase() (players.c) - a player already at the cap can still
  acquire a 4th undeveloped property by winning it at auction, since
  runAuction()/decideAuctionBid() don't check economy.antiSpeculationActive.
