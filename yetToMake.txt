yetToMake.txt
==============
What still needs to be built so the whole MONOPOLY-LK simulation actually runs, not
just the pieces that currently exist in isolation. Organized by file. Updated after
finishing all 6 events.c functions (drawNationalEventCard, drawRegionalDevelopmentCard,
inflationChange, propertyMarketChange, governmentRegulationChange, triggerDisaster)
and drafting a fixed runAuction() in roadmap/ideaflow.txt's game.c section.

- board.c    - board setup + movement done; rent-by-development-level missing
- finance.c  - loans/insurance/rent/net-worth done; several hooks still unwired
- events.c   - DONE: all 6 functions implemented and internally correct
- players.c  - EMPTY (0 bytes) - none of Section 3's strategy logic exists
- game.c     - EMPTY (0 bytes) - no turn loop, so nothing above ever actually runs
- main.c     - EMPTY (0 bytes) - no entry point, project can't link into a runnable

game.c  (currently EMPTY - 0 bytes)
------------------------------------
The biggest remaining gap. Every events.c function is finished and correct, but
nothing calls any of them yet - this file is what's supposed to call them on
schedule. A draft turn sequence and runAuction() already exist in
roadmap/ideaflow.txt's "## 8. game.c" section; still need to write the real file.
- Turn loop implementing Rule 3's sequence (resolve penalties, roll dice, move,
  resolve landing action, purchase, construct, financial transactions, end turn).
- Player order determination (Rule 2 - roll dice, highest goes first, reroll ties).
- Landing-action dispatch by square type (Table 1): must call
  drawNationalEventCard() when a player lands on an Event square (squares 2, 7, 22,
  36), and runAuction() when a purchase is declined (Rule 5).
- A round counter and "end of round" step that:
    - calls applyLoanInterest() (finance.c) - already exists, just needs wiring in
    - calls inflationChange() and propertyMarketChange() every 10 rounds
    - calls triggerDisaster() every 10 rounds
    - calls drawRegionalDevelopmentCard() every 15 rounds
    - calls governmentRegulationChange() every 20 rounds
    - decrements every ActiveEffect's roundsRemaining (nationalCard per player,
      regionalCard/governmentRegulation/marketBoom/marketDecline on economy) and,
      when one hits 0, reverts whatever it changed (see the Rule-LK 35 note under
      finance.c below - this needs a design decision, not just a loop)
    - prints the Round N Summary block (Section 5 "End of Every Round") and the
      Rule-LK 36 "Current Market Conditions" block
- Bankruptcy detection (Rule 14) and win-condition checking (Rule 15: one solvent
  player left, or 500 rounds reached).
- The separate Section 2.5 "Economic Events" mechanic (Rule-LK 18: Tourism Boom,

  needs its own function and its own "every 15 rounds" trigger.

finance.c
-----------
- payRent() never checks square->isDamaged. Heavy Floods / Political Rally /
  National Disaster / triggerDisaster() mark a property isDamaged = 1, but rent
  still gets collected in full - Rule-LK 11 ("damaged buildings cannot collect rent
  until repairs are completed") isn't enforced anywhere.
- No repair mechanism exists to clear isDamaged once a player has sufficient funds
  (Rule-LK 11's "repairs are automatically performed") for disasters that weren't
  paid off immediately.
- getInsurance() computes its premium as a hardcoded 5% / 10% / 15% of property
  value. There's no discount input, so "Insurance Discount" (National Event Card)
  and "Insurance Regulation" (Government Regulation) have nowhere to apply their
  -20%/-15% - both are marked as still-to-do directly in events.c.
- No Income Tax collection function exists at all (Rule 11) - and there's no stored
  Income Tax amount anywhere either (see types.h below), so "Increase Property Tax"
  (Government Regulation) has nothing to increase by 50%.
- No dice-based utility rent calculation exists (Section 1.1.3: 4x dice value for
  one utility owned, 10x for both). Utility squares' currentRent is fixed at 0 in
  board.c and nothing ever recalculates it, so Power Failure / Electricity Tariff
  Increase / Water Shortage / Electricity Tariff Revision currently multiply 0 and
  stay 0.
- No railway rent-by-station-count calculation exists (Table 2: 250/500/1000/2000
  depending on how many stations the same player owns). currentRent for railways is
  a flat 250 set once in board.c, so Fuel Shortage / Transport Strike / Port
  Expansion / Railway Modernization currently just multiply that flat number
  instead of the correct ownership-scaled value.
- No "round tick" function exists that:
    - ages every property (Rule-LK 15) and applies depreciation past 50 rounds
      (Rule-LK 16)
    - decays building condition by 2%/round (Rule-LK 25) and closes buildings
      below 25% condition (Rule-LK 26)
    - counts down insuranceRoundsLeft and expires policies (Rule-LK 9)
    - reverts a Regional/National/Regulation/Market effect's currentValue/
      currentRent change once its roundsRemaining reaches 0 (Rule-LK 35) - needs a
      design decision (see the types.h baseline note below), not just a loop
    - repeats the Luxury Property Tax charge every round while that regulation is
      active, instead of the one-off charge governmentRegulationChange() currently
      applies (marked ASSUMPTION in events.c)

types.h
---------
- No Income Tax amount anywhere. board.c's Income Tax square (index 4) only sets
  name/type/group/ownerId - there's no constant or field for how much it actually
  costs. Needed for Rule 11 and for the "Increase Property Tax" regulation to have
  something to increase by 50%. Suggest a constant (e.g. INCOME_TAX_AMOUNT) plus a
  mutable economy.currentIncomeTaxAmount initialized from it, since the regulation
  needs to change it without touching the compile-time constant.
- No "insurance premium discount" field on Economy for Insurance Discount / Insurance
  Regulation to set and for finance.c's getInsurance() to read.
- No "construction suspended" flag/counter on Economy for Labour Strike to set and
  for players.c to check before building.
- No field to track a temporary square-level closure duration. Political Rally
  ("one random property closed for 2 rounds") currently just sets isDamaged = 1
  with no way to know when those 2 rounds are up and reopen it automatically.
  Suggest something like int closedRoundsLeft on Square.
- No per-group cooldown tracker for Rule-LK 33. lastBoomGroup/lastDeclineGroup on
  Economy only remember the single most recent group each, which is enough to stop
  an immediate repeat (what propertyMarketChange() currently enforces) but not
  enough for the full "can't be reselected for 30 rounds" rule across all 8 groups.
  Suggest something like int groupCooldownRounds[9] on Economy.
- No stored per-square "baseline" value/rent to revert to once an effect's
  roundsRemaining expires (Rule-LK 35). Right now currentValue/currentRent are the
  only record kept, and every card/regulation/market effect permanently multiplies
  them in place - since Rule-LK 34 lets several percentage changes stack on the same
  square, there's no way to cleanly undo just the one that expired without also
  knowing what the square looked like before it hit. This is a bigger design
  decision (e.g. an immutable snapshot of the board taken at startup, recomputing
  current* fields each round from baseline x every still-active multiplier) rather
  than a one-line field addition - worth deciding on purpose before building it.
- economy.regionalCard/nationalCard/governmentRegulation only store a numeric
  effectId, not which card/regulation is active - so Rule-LK 36's "Current Market
  Conditions" display (which needs to print the card's *name*, e.g. "Northern
  Development Programme (+30%)") has nothing to read that name from once the
  card/regulation has moved on. Would need either a stored name/id lookup at
  display time, or a name field added directly to ActiveEffect.
