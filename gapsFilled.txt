gapsFilled.txt
================
A log of gaps that were identified (either by a full requirements audit against
the assignment document, or by a lecturer clarification) and then actually
fixed in the code. Each entry says what was wrong, what the fix was, and
which files changed.

This is different from assumptions.txt (judgment calls filling a silent gap
in the brief) and yetToMake.txt (things still not built at all). This file
is specifically a changelog of "found broken/missing -> now fixed."


1. Income Tax was a flat, invented LKR 200 - now 15% of cash on hand
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Lecturer clarification - "Income tax base rate at the beginning of
the game is 15%. Market conditions will affect the rate as well."

What was wrong: Rule 11 never gives an amount, so an earlier pass invented a
flat LKR 200 (documented as assumption #26 at the time). The lecturer's
clarification makes clear it should be a percentage rate starting at 15%,
not a flat number.

Fix: economy.currentIncomeTaxAmount (a flat LKR figure) was renamed to
economy.currentIncomeTaxPercent and reworked to charge 15% of the player's
current cash on hand. Cash (not net worth or property value) was chosen as
the base specifically to keep this tax structurally different from the
Community Development Fund tax below (which the lecturer explicitly bases
on property value) - if both taxes used the same base they'd be redundant.
The existing "Increase Property Tax" Government Regulation (+50%) still
works exactly as before, just multiplying a percentage now instead of a
flat amount.

Files changed: types.h (INCOME_TAX_AMOUNT -> INCOME_TAX_STARTING_PERCENT,
Economy.currentIncomeTaxAmount -> currentIncomeTaxPercent), finance.c
(collectTax()), events.c (Increase Property Tax regulation), game.c
(starting value seeded in runGame()).


2. Community Development Fund had no effect of its own - now a distinct 10%
   property-value tax, separate from the National Event Card deck
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Lecturer clarification - "Community development fund will levy a tax
on the player whom lands on the cell. The tax is 10% of the total assets of
the player. For the purpose of assets the current market rate of only the
property (not buildings) is considered. 10% will also be affected by the
market fluctuations."

What was wrong: Table 1 lists Community Development Fund (square 2) as an
"Event" type square, the exact same type as the three squares literally
named "National Event Card" (7, 22, 36). Before this fix, all four Event
squares ran through the same switch case in playTurn() and drew from the
same 20-card Appendix A deck - Community Development Fund had no code path
of its own at all.

Fix: playTurn()'s SQUARE_EVENT case now checks squareIndex == 2 specifically
and calls a new function, applyCommunityDevelopmentFundTax(), instead of
drawNationalEventCard(). That function sums currentValue across every
SQUARE_PROPERTY square the landing player owns (railways and utilities are
excluded - "property" is read as Table 1's literal Property category, not
every owned asset) and charges economy.communityFundTaxPercent (10%,
starting value) of that total. "Not buildings" needed no extra work: this
codebase's currentValue was already established (assumptions.txt #5) as a
single combined number with no separate building-value component, so
excluding buildings was already the default. "10% affected by market
fluctuations" was read as the RESULTING TAX AMOUNT changing because the
property values it's computed from already fluctuate with inflation/cards/
market events (all of which already modify currentValue) - not as the 10%
figure itself needing a dedicated trigger, since no rule names one. The
percentage is still stored as a mutable Economy field rather than a literal
constant, so nothing stops a future regulation from adjusting it directly if
one is ever added.

Files changed: types.h (new communityFundTaxPercent field,
COMMUNITY_FUND_TAX_PERCENT constant, applyCommunityDevelopmentFundTax()
prototype), finance.c (the new function), game.c (SQUARE_EVENT dispatch in
playTurn(), starting value seeded in runGame()).


3. Railway station purchase price didn't match utilities (2,500 instead of
   1,500)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Direct user correction - railway and utility purchase prices should
both be LKR 1,500.

What was wrong: assumptions.txt entry #1 (still true in spirit - neither
price is given in the brief) had picked 2,500 for railways using
Light-Blue-group reasoning, while utilities (entry #2) used 1,500 matching
the cheapest colour group. The user corrected this: both should be the same
1,500 figure.

Fix: all 4 railway squares' purchasePrice/currentValue changed 2500 -> 1500,
and mortgageValue changed 1250 -> 750 to preserve the existing 50%-of-
purchase-price ratio used everywhere else on the board.

Files changed: board.c (all 4 SQUARE_RAILWAY definitions).


4. payRent() never printed anything - silently moved cash with no output
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Found while verifying railway/utility rent-payment logic per user
request. Applies to every square type, not just railways/utilities.

What was wrong: Section 5 (Required Output Messages) explicitly requires a
"landed on / Rent Paid / Owner" message every time rent changes hands, but
payRent() (finance.c) had no printf at all - the cash correctly moved, but
silently, for every property, railway, and utility rent payment in the
whole simulation.

Fix: added the exact Section 5 format to the end of payRent(), right after
the cash transfer:
    X landed on Y.
    Rent Paid : LKR Z.
    Owner : W.

Files changed: finance.c (payRent()).


5. Rule-LK 27-29 (maintenance system) was entirely unbuilt
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Full requirements audit.

What was wrong: building condition only ever decayed 2%/round with nothing
to restore it, so every developed property permanently hit 0% condition
(rent-dead, Table 3) well before round 500. Neither routine maintenance nor
the 20-round neglect penalty existed at all.

Fix: added performMaintenance() (restores condition to 100%, cost 5%/8% of
house/hotel cost) picked once per turn by decidePerformMaintenance() (worst
condition, affordable, first - no strategy states a rule so this is
strategy-neutral). Added applyStructuralDamage(), a round tick that tracks
roundsSinceMaintenance per square and applies a one-off penalty (value -15%,
rent -25%, future maintenance cost +50%) once it exceeds
MAINTENANCE_NEGLECT_LIMIT (20). Added renovateStructuralDamage() (Rule-LK 29)
to reverse that penalty for 25% of the building's replacement cost, picked by
decideRenovateStructuralDamage() as a step-7 financial transaction.

Bug caught while verifying: the new maintenanceCostPercent field defaulted
to 0 (C zero-fills struct fields board.c's designated initializers don't
set), not 100 - meaning every maintenance charge computed as (cost * 0) /
100 = 0, so maintenance was completely free the first time it was built.
Fixed by adding a loop at the end of monopolyBoard() that sets it to 100 for
every square. Caught via an isolated synthetic test that manually forced
the neglect threshold and checked the actual numbers, not just whether the
code compiled.

Files changed: types.h (3 new Square fields, MAINTENANCE_NEGLECT_LIMIT was
already defined but unused), board.c (maintenanceCostPercent init loop),
finance.c (3 new functions), players.c (2 new decide functions), game.c
(maintenance wired at the very start of playTurn(), structural renovation
wired into step 7).


6. Rule-LK 17 (depreciation renovation) was entirely unbuilt
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Full requirements audit.

What was wrong: depreciationPercent was tracked (Rule-LK 15/16) but nothing
ever let a player renovate it away, despite Rule-LK 17 and two strategies
(Section 3.2, 3.4) explicitly describing this action.

Fix: added renovateDepreciation() (finance.c) - restores currentValue and
currentRent by dividing back out depreciationPercent, resets propertyAge,
costs 10% of currentValue. decideRenovateDepreciation() (players.c) uses
each strategy's own stated threshold where one exists (Conservative >10%,
Opportunistic >15%), and a documented ASSUMPTION for the two strategies with
no stated rule (Risk Taker: waits for the 30% cap, matching "ignores
depreciation until repair becomes unavoidable"; Aggressive: renovates as
soon as any depreciation exists, matching its rent-focused philosophy).
Wired into playTurn()'s landing action - only triggers when a player lands
on a property they already own, matching Rule-LK 17's literal wording.

Files changed: finance.c, players.c, game.c.


7. Rule-LK 6 foreclosure didn't demolish buildings or cancel insurance
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Full requirements audit.

What was wrong: applyLoanInterest()'s foreclosure branch only transferred
ownership back to the Bank and cleared the loan lock - numHouses, hasHotel,
and insurancePolicy were left stale on squares seized as collateral, even
though Rule-LK 6 explicitly lists both as happening during foreclosure.

Fix: added the same numHouses/hasHotel/insurancePolicy/insuranceRoundsLeft
reset checkBankruptcy() already used, right before each collateral square
is released.

Files changed: finance.c (applyLoanInterest()).


8. Rule-LK 5's "extend loan period" and "increase loan amount" didn't exist
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Full requirements audit.

What was wrong: landing on the Bank while already holding a loan only ever
offered repayment - two of Rule-LK 5's four listed actions were missing
entirely.

Fix: added extendLoan() (resets loanRoundsLeft to 20) and increaseLoan()
(borrows more, pledging any still-unlocked eligible collateral the same way
takeLoan() does). decideLoanExtension() is strategy-neutral (ASSUMPTION: a
player extends once nearly due and unable to clear the balance).
decideLoanIncrease() is Risk-Taker-only, directly matching Section 3.3's
"Frequently refinances loans to increase available capital" - no other
strategy has a stated rule for this, so they don't do it. Wired into
SQUARE_BANK with a clear priority order: repay, then extend, then increase,
so only one financial transaction happens per landing (Section 1.1.4).

Files changed: finance.c, players.c, game.c.


9. Bankrupt/foreclosed assets were never auctioned (Section 2.6)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Full requirements audit.

What was wrong: Section 2.6 explicitly lists "a bankrupt player's assets are
liquidated" and "foreclosed assets return to the Bank" as auction triggers,
but checkBankruptcy() and the foreclosure branch of applyLoanInterest() both
just set ownerId = -1 directly - never auctioned.

Fix: both now call runAuction() for each square right after stripping its
buildings/insurance, instead of leaving it unowned outright. Since
isBankrupt is already set to 1 before the bankrupt player's own squares are
auctioned, runAuction()'s existing "bankrupt players don't bid" rule (Rule
6) correctly keeps them out of their own liquidation.

Files changed: finance.c (checkBankruptcy(), applyLoanInterest()).


10. Market/card events only changed currentValue, never purchasePrice
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Lecturer clarification ("purchase price... will be affected by the
market and other conditions") plus the full requirements audit.

What was wrong: the actual purchase charge (game.c) reads purchasePrice, but
every National Event Card, Regional Development Card, and Economic Event
that changes a "value" (as opposed to a "rent") only touched currentValue -
so most of these systems had no effect on what a property actually cost to
buy. Only Inflation and Market Boom already touched purchasePrice correctly.

Fix: added a matching purchasePrice line next to every currentValue "value
effect" line across all three card systems (13 spots total). Left Market
Decline alone - Rule-LK 32 explicitly doesn't list a purchase price effect
for it, only Market Boom does (Rule-LK 31), so that asymmetry is correct as
written, not a gap.

Files changed: events.c (drawNationalEventCard(), drawRegionalDevelopmentCard(),
drawEconomicEvent()).


11. Rule-LK 32's -25% auction starting price wasn't wired in
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Full requirements audit (was already a TODO comment in the code).

Fix: runAuction()'s opening bid now checks if the square's group is the
active Market Decline's target group, and if so cuts the 50%-of-value
opening bid by a further 25%.

Files changed: game.c (runAuction()).


12. Anti-Speculation Act's "develop within 5 rounds" wasn't enforced
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Full requirements audit.

What was wrong: only the 3-undeveloped-property purchase cap existed. The
second half of the rule ("Additional purchases require immediate
development within five rounds") had no code at all.

Fix: added developmentDeadlineRounds to Square, set to 5 whenever a property
is purchased while the Act is active, and also set for every already-owned
undeveloped property the moment the Act first activates. A round-tick
function (enforceDevelopmentDeadlines()) counts it down and, if it hits 0
while still undeveloped, seizes the property and auctions it off - reusing
the same liquidation pattern as bankruptcy/foreclosure (#9) rather than
inventing a new penalty mechanism. Building on the square before the
deadline clears it.

Files changed: types.h, events.c (deadline set on Act activation), finance.c
(enforceDevelopmentDeadlines(), added to the round tick), game.c (deadline
set on purchase, cleared on construction).


13. Rule 13's jail release choice was forced/identical for every strategy
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Direct user question ("is it implemented according to his strategy?").

What was wrong: Rule 13 lists three ways out of Jail (pay bail, roll doubles,
wait 3 turns), but the code only ever rolled doubles each turn and forced a
bail payment on the 3rd failed attempt - identical for all four players.
Section 3's "player shall evaluate all legal actions and execute the one
that best satisfies its behavioural strategy" was not applied to jail at
all, and no assumption for this was recorded anywhere.

Fix: added decideJailAction() (players.c), called at the top of the jail
check each turn. ASSUMPTION, given by the user directly: Aggressive Investor
and Risk Taker pay bail immediately if affordable, falling back to rolling
doubles if not; Conservative Banker pays only once his cash is at or above
the average cash of all 4 players; Opportunistic Trader pays only while a
profitable Market Boom (Rule-LK 30/31) is active. Any strategy whose
condition isn't met simply waits, and the existing "3 turns then forced
bail" fallback still applies underneath so nobody stays jailed forever.

Files changed: types.h (decideJailAction() prototype), players.c (the new
function), game.c (jail-check block in playTurn() restructured to call it).


14. decideJailOrOut() refined: Conservative and Opportunistic also roll
    doubles as a fallback, not just wait
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Direct user correction to entry #13's first version.

What was wrong: only Aggressive Investor and Risk Taker fell back to rolling
doubles when their bail condition wasn't met - Conservative Banker and
Opportunistic Trader just waited straight through to the forced-bail
fallback, skipping Rule 13's doubles option entirely.

Fix: the doubles roll in game.c's jail block is no longer restricted to two
strategies - every strategy rolls if it didn't pay bail this turn. Also
tightened Conservative Banker's condition to compare cash AFTER paying
bail (cash - BAIL_AMOUNT) against the player average, not cash before
paying, so he never pays himself below the pack.

Files changed: players.c (decideJailOrOut()), game.c (jail-check block).


15. Disaster repair cost was a flat 10% for every disaster type, and never
    counted houses/hotels
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Direct user request to tier the repair cost by disaster severity and
include buildings in the damage base.

What was wrong: triggerDisaster() charged the same 10% of currentValue no
matter which of the 5 disaster types struck, and currentValue never actually
reflects the houses/hotel built on a square (a known gap, assumptions.txt
#5) - so a disaster on a hotel-covered property repaired for exactly the
same cost as one on bare land.

Fix: repair cost now scales by disaster severity - Electrical Failure 5%,
Riot 10%, Flood 15%, Fire 20%, Building Collapse 30% (matches
DEPRECIATION_MAX_PERCENTAGE, the document's own maximum-damage figure) -
and is calculated off (currentValue + the square's house/hotel replacement
value), not currentValue alone. Compensation percentages (80%/100% by
policy) are unchanged, since they multiply whatever repairCost comes out to.

Files changed: events.c (triggerDisaster()), assumptions.txt (#13 updated).


16. decidePerformMaintenance()/decideLoanExtension() gave Risk Taker the
    same behavior as every other strategy, despite having stated traits
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Direct user request, following up on the "optional strategy
differentiation" candidates listed in yetToMake.txt.

What was wrong: Rule-LK 27 maintenance and Rule-LK 5 loan extension were
strategy-neutral for all 4 players, even though Risk Taker has directly
applicable stated traits elsewhere in Section 3.3 ("ignores property
depreciation until repair becomes unavoidable," "frequently refinances
loans to increase available capital") that were never extended to these
two mechanics.

Fix: decidePerformMaintenance() (renamed to decideToMaintain() per user
request) now only maintains once a Risk Taker's building condition drops
to 25% (Table 3's "Building Closed" cutoff) instead of maintaining
proactively at any condition below 100%. decideLoanExtension() now lets
Risk Taker extend proactively any time loanRoundsLeft <= 15, instead of
only when nearly due (<=5) AND unable to fully repay. Both changes are
Risk-Taker-only; the other three strategies are untouched.

decideRenovateStructuralDamage() was deliberately left strategy-neutral -
structural damage is a one-time on/off flag, not a graded value like
building condition, so there's no existing field to threshold a "neglect
until unavoidable" rule against without inventing new state just for this.

Files changed: types.h (rename), players.c (decideToMaintain(),
decideLoanExtension()), game.c (call site renamed).
